How we make it

Four decisions that do the heavy lifting

None of this is a secret, and none of it is complicated. It is just more expensive than the alternative, so most of the market skips it.

Nickel-free brass, always. The base metal is where skin reactions start. We pay more for it and we do not swap it out when the price moves.

24Kt plating, measured. Every batch is checked for plating thickness, not just colour. Thin plating looks identical on day one and nothing like it on day ninety.

A sealing coat over the plating. This is the step that gets cut. It is what stands between your necklace and the air.

Six months of wear testing. Real wear, on real people, before a piece gets a product page. Some designs never make it past this.

What we will not do

Three things, plainly

We do not run fake countdown timers or invented "original" prices. The struck-through price on every page is a price the piece has actually sold at.

We do not make a piece we would not wear through a full working day. If it is heavy enough to hurt by evening, it does not ship.

We do not argue about returns inside the seven days. It costs us less to take the piece back than to keep a customer who feels stuck with it.
